# Web Please brand system

## Brand idea

**The direct route to the web.** The mark pairs an orange starting point with a clear forward path. It represents a search moving directly to Web results before an AI Overview can take over the page.

## Positioning

- Product promise: Skip AI Overviews and open Google Web results automatically.
- Primary line: **Straight to the web.**
- Supporting line: **Skip the AI Overview. Keep the links.**
- Voice: direct, calm, useful, and non-ideological.

## Visual system

- Route Black: `#181818`
- Action Orange: `#FF6B0B`
- Warm Paper: `#FBFAF7`
- Cool Canvas: `#F2F2F4`
- Soft Line: `#E4E4E7`
- Muted Ink: `#686868`

The high-contrast neutral system and single orange action accent take inspiration from Sketch & Scale's visual language. Route Black carries the brand; Action Orange is reserved for the starting point and primary actions. Warm Paper keeps the extension quieter than a typical utility UI.

## Logo rules

- Keep the arrow and starting point together; neither is a standalone logo.
- Use the icon with at least 12.5% clear space on every side.
- Do not recolor it with Google's four-color palette.
- Do not add gradients, shadows, outlines, or an anti-AI prohibition symbol.
